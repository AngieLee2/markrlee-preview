Counsel for the airport authority

Napa County v. 3030 Airport Rd., LLC

A hangar lease barred the tenant LLC from leasing space for aircraft it did not own. The tenant leased space to many outside owners anyway, arguing that they had become nominal “members” of the LLC. Professor Lee was retained to consult and testify on whether “members” with no equity interest or voting rights qualified as members at all.

Outcome: The airport authority won possession, several hundred thousand dollars in damages, and attorney’s fees.
